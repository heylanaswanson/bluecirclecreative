A way to get paid for the thing you're already good at.

You've spent years building a real ear — finding tracks 500 people have heard, mixing, and setting sound to moving image. The trick isn't becoming an influencer. It's selling the work, not yourself. There's a whole side of music where nobody needs to see your face — and it rewards exactly your taste: emotional, melodic electronic with vocals on top — vocal trance at the core, melodic techno close behind.

The 12-month spine
Months 1–2 · Plumbing
Register with a PRO (BMI = free) + SoundExchange (free). Build a small catalog aimed at sync. Cost: ~$0.
Months 2–5 · Get it out there
Submit to sync libraries (mostly commission, not upfront). List your first 1–2 packs on Gumroad / BeatStars / Splice. Cost: near $0.
Months 4–9 · Take the gigs
Ghost production + direct client work via SoundBetter, Airbit, Fiverr Pro, producer Discords. This is the bigger checks.
Months 9–12 · Double down
Pour energy into whichever lane is actually converting. Stop spreading thin. Compound the winner.

Where to start — bookmark these

Royalties (do first, free)

  • bmi.com — register as songwriter, free
  • soundexchange.com — collects the recording royalty PROs don't

Sync libraries

  • artlist.io · epidemicsound.com
  • musicbed.com · marmosetmusic.com
  • songtradr.com — sync marketplace

Sell sounds & packs

  • splice.com — samples & presets, curated
  • gumroad.com — sell direct, free to start
  • beatstars.com — beats & tracks

Gigs & ghost production

  • soundbetter.com — pro production/mixing marketplace
  • airbit.com · fiverr.com (Pro)
  • Producer Discords + r/WeAreTheMusicMakers for leads

One honest note: sync and royalties are slow to start — placements take months, royalties trickle before they flow. The faster money is packs and ghost production. So if "change my life this year" needs to mean income this year, start there — and let sync compound quietly in the background as the long game.
